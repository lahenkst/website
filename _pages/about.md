I am a Research Associate at the Mannheim Centre for European Social Research and a PhD Candidate at the Graduate School for Economics and Social Sciences at the University of Mannheim. Currently I am part of the DFG-funded research project "Group Appeals in Electoral and Parliamentary Debates (GAEPD) which is lead by Dr. Lena Huber and Ass.-Prof. Dr. Hauke Licht.

Previously I obtained my master degree in empirical democracy studies from the University of Mannheim. I received my bachelor  degree in Political Science and Cultural Anthropology from the University of Göttingen and studied abroad at Amherst College, Massachussetts for two semesters.
